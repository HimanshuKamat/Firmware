# Spark Sheet: Kids' exercise adventure   (Gate 1: DECIDED, angle A)

Idea (owner's words): > i want to create a video for kids to exercise. simple exercises that kids can do and follow making it interesting so they keep watching it. video should be 15-30 mins. timer counter. story based
Cast: one girl, Tisha, aged 3-4, speaking to the viewer. Animated. Veo 3.1 Fast or Lite via the Gemini API (to be tested).

## Specialist inputs
Pending: Movement Designer and Cultural Advisor (waiting for the engagement and movement research); Trend Scout input is in `research/market_trends.md` (see the idea brief). This sheet will be updated when they report.

## Shared structure for every angle (20-minute master, cut to 15 or grown to 30)
Welcome and "rules of the game" (1:00) > warm-up (2:00) > four chapters of about 3:45 > cool-down with breathing (1:30) > goodbye and a promise of the next adventure (0:30). In each chapter: about 45 s of story, three exercises of about 50 s, a short transition. Each chapter has its OWN problem and its OWN twist so the series is never a template.
Timer and counter (graphics, free): a visible countdown ring per exercise, a big rep counter ("1, 2, 3 ..."), and a journey progress device that fills as the film goes. A "ready, steady, go!" and a "well done!" sparkle every time.

Brainstorm moves used: child's job first, movement verb first, moral by action, rule of three, the viewer as buddy, season hook.

## Recommendation
**Angle A, "Tisha and the Glowing Egg", as the pilot.** It has a clear goal a 3-year-old understands (get the egg home), a built-in timer (the egg glow), animals that map to simple moves, different obstacles per chapter, a reward (the hatching), and the easiest production (one child, one animal, nature). Idea to carry over from the others: end with a calm sleepy cool-down from D, and put a toy-parade surprise at the end of the egg chapter from B only if the owner wants it.
