# Respect and Accuracy Note: "Tisha and the Glowing Egg" (angle A)

Cultural and Values Advisor, 2026-10-07. Tags: [S] source-backed, [I] my inference, [U] could not verify; "weak" = snippet or secondary page. I am not a religious or legal authority. A human family reviewer must sign off (section 8). The search tool is US-only, so India-specific evidence is thin.

**Status: ORIGINAL STORY.** Invented for this film. It is not a myth, folktale or festival story, it is not set in Mooshak's world, and no deity appears. Never call any part of it "traditional" or "an Indian legend". Description wording: "an original story". No festival is used, so the festival and date check does not apply. If the go-live is near Diwali (8 Nov 2026), keep diyas, rangoli and Lakshmi imagery out entirely.

**Verdict: proceed, with six conditions.**
1. A "story egg" frame, so the film never models touching real eggs (2c).
2. An accurate peahen and peachick (2a).
3. Footprints and glow that do not echo sacred imagery (3c).
4. Tisha never plays at the water's edge (6).
5. No named dance form; one gesture only (5).
6. Family reviewer sign-off, plus a name check on "Tisha" (1, 4).

## 2. Wildlife accuracy and values

**a. Peafowl.** Male = peacock, female = peahen, baby = peachick, group = peafowl [S] https://pbs.org/wnet/nature/blog/peacock-fact-sheet

| Fact | Film rule |
|---|---|
| Nest is a shallow scrape on the ground, lined with leaves and sticks [S] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_peafowl | The nest is a cosy ground hollow under a bush or in tall grass, not a twig basket in a tree. This fits the "bend and gather leaves" move. |
| Clutch 3-8 eggs; incubation 28-30 days [S] PBS, Wikipedia | At the end, show two or three eggs in the nest, so the film does not say "one egg per mother". |
| Eggs are pale white to buff or reddish-brown, about 6-7.5 cm [S] Wikipedia | The shell is cream-buff under the glow, not blue or green. The glow is magic and soft. |
| The peahen is brown with a crest and an iridescent green neck [S] Wikipedia; the peachick is downy buff-brown [S weak] https://cosleyzoo.org/india-blue-peafowl/ | The mother is brown and the chick is buff, not a mini peacock with a train. Make "Mama is brown!" the gentle reveal ("brown helps her hide in the grass"). |
| Peachicks are precocial: they walk and follow the mother almost at once [S] Wikipedia | At hatching the mother is already beside the egg. Never show the chick following Tisha. |
| The male does not help raise chicks [S] PBS | Do not cast the peacock as "Papa". If he appears, he is a distant background bird. |
| Peafowl are India's national bird and Schedule I (highest protection) under the Wild Life (Protection) Act 1972 [S] https://www.indiaenvironmentportal.org.in/news-clippings/moef-asks-states-to-protect-peacocks | See 2c. |

Use "peachick" once, as a fun word. "Baby peacock" is acceptable colloquially, but the narrator says "peahen" once for Mama.

**b. The four lands.** They are all plausible in India but not in one place or one season. Frogs and peafowl breeding follow the monsoon; flamingos are mostly winter visitors (December to May) [S weak] https://www.outlooktraveller.com/experiences/nature/winter-wildlife-top-places-to-spot-flamingoes-in-india . So frame the route as a storybook walk and never as a real journey.

| Land | Realism | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Frog pond | Indian bullfrog and skipper frog live in ponds and paddy [S weak] https://roundglasssustain.com/species/bullfrogs-of-india | Pond edge, shallow, and never wading (section 6). |
| Elephant forest | Wild Asian elephants live in forests, mostly Western Ghats and the Northeast [S] https://india.mongabay.com/short-article/2026/09/the-largest-land-mammal-in-the-country/ | A free elephant with no rider, chain, mahout or painted forehead. A study of captive elephants in Karnataka found poor conditions, including chaining and use for riding [S weak] https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/captive-elephants-languishing-pathetic-condition8200study-2167632 , so a working or temple elephant shown as normal would hurt. |
| Monkey trees | Three common species: rhesus (north and central, often near towns), bonnet (south), common or Hanuman langur (widespread, leaf-eating) [S weak] https://indiasendangered.com/monkeys-of-india/ | Use the **common langur** (grey, black face, long tail). Treat it kindly: it is revered in Hindu tradition [I]. No food-snatching or egg-snatching (a stereotype; langurs are mostly leaf-eaters), no "jumping on the bed". |
| Flamingo lake | Greater flamingos breed in the Rann of Kutch and winter at Pulicat and Sambhar; both greater and lesser occur in Gujarat, Rajasthan and Mumbai [S weak] https://datazone.birdlife.org/site/factsheet/sambhar-lake-iba-india , https://indianbirds.in/pdfs/IB_15_5_Parasharya_Gadhvi_GreaterFlamingoBreeding.pdf | Name no real place. A salt-lake setting is enough. |

**c. Teaching "we leave real eggs alone".** Real eggs are best left where they are, and an adult can call a wildlife expert [S weak, US sources] https://wildlife.utah.gov/news/utah-wildlife-news/1167-what-to-do-if-you-find-a-baby-bird-on-the-ground-or-a-nest-with-eggs-in-it.html ; no India-specific guidance found [U]. In India the Act's definition of "hunting" includes "disturbing the eggs or nests" of wild birds [S, via search snippet; direct fetch blocked] https://sherloc.unodc.org/cld/zh/legislation/ind/the_wildlife_protection_act_1972/chapter_i/article_1-2.html . A child carrying a peahen's egg is therefore the one behaviour the film must not make look normal. Four layers:
1. **Rule in the welcome (about 5 s).** The narrator says: "This is a story egg, made of sparkles. If you ever find a real egg, leave it where it is and tell a grown-up." Make it one of the "rules of the game".
2. **Story logic.** The egg is magic: it glows and taps, it asks for help, and a gentle breeze puffs it out of the nest. Nobody is at fault, and the peahen is never blamed.
3. **Contrast line.** "Real eggs don't glow and don't ask for rides."
4. **Ending as action.** Mama takes over, Tisha steps back and waves, and nothing is kept. The narrator repeats the rule in the goodbye.

## 3. Indian setting and representation

**a. Tisha.** Warm medium-brown skin; black hair in two bunches or a plait; a plain cotton tunic or kurta top with leggings or shorts, in bright solid colours; bare feet or plain socks. No bindi, tilak or nazar tika by default, so the film does not mark one religion for a character of no stated faith [I]. Minimal jewellery. AI image models skew stereotyped and lighter for Indian subjects [S] https://restofworld.org/2023/ai-image-stereotypes/ , so state the skin tone in the character block and check every shot.

**b. Home and garden.** A veranda or courtyard garden, or a building-complex garden, since many families live in flats [I]. Plants: banyan, neem, mango, marigold (genda), jasmine (mogra), hibiscus, a pond with water lilies. Shoes off indoors. No parents on screen: the voice says "your grown-up", not mummy, nani or aaji, so single-parent, grandparent and diaspora homes all fit. No saree on a toddler, no palace, temple or Taj backdrop, no snake charmers, no "saffron everything" palette, no head-wobble or mock accent in any voice.

**c. Do not use religious imagery casually.**
- Footprints can read as Lakshmi's *shripada*, drawn at doors and pointing inward [S weak] https://www.boldsky.com/yoga-spirituality/faith-mysticism/2013/significance-of-lakshmi-footprints-035947.html . So make them Tisha's own bare child footprints, or animal tracks, in soft teal-and-gold (the bible palette), glowing, never red kumkum, never in neat pairs aimed at a doorway.
- A golden glowing egg echoes the Hindu "golden egg" (Hiranyagarbha, Brahmanda) of Vedic cosmology [S] https://hinduwebsite.com/symbolism/symbols/egg.asp . So make the glow soft pearl-white and warm, with rainbow sparkles. No lotus under the egg, no pedestal or altar framing, no garland on the egg.
- Lotus: no stepping or standing on an open lotus; use plain green leaf-pads.
- Tulsi, if shown, is background only: never stepped on, never an exercise obstacle.
- No rangoli or kolam under exercising feet. No deity-printed mats or rugs (movement research 6).
- The film's peafowl are wild birds, not Kartikeya's mount, so no crossover.

**d. Gender and family.** Tisha is active and brave. Do not equate "girl" with "caring for a baby": the narrator says "everyone helps". The peahen as solo parent is accurate. No pink-only palette.

## 5. Movement culture: one named Indian form?

**Recommendation: NO named dance or classical form in this film (no garba, no bhangra, no mudra names). YES to one gesture, "thank-you hands" (hands together), used once in the goodbye.**
- Garba is a devotional circle dance for the goddess, UNESCO-listed in 2023 [S] https://www.governancenow.com/news/regular-story/garba-of-gujarat-now-a-unesco-intangible-cultural-heritage . Bhangra is a Punjabi harvest dance [S weak] https://ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bhangra . Neither fits a peafowl-egg story, and each should be taught by a practitioner, not borrowed as a "generic Indian dance" [I].
- Name the animal moves plainly ("peacock arms", "elephant trunk"), not "mayura" or "gaja hasta", because that would claim a tradition we did not research [I].
- "Thank-you hands" is an everyday South Asian greeting and thanks. Say "hands together, say thank you", and "namaste" once only if the reviewer agrees (families who say salaam or sat sri akal exist) [I].
- Do not call the cool-down "yoga". The Hindu American Foundation asks that yoga be credited to its Hindu roots [S] https://www.npr.org/2012/04/11/150352063/to-some-hindus-modern-yoga-has-lost-its-way . Say "belly breaths".
- Garba or bhangra could suit a later festival film, with a named human consultant.

## 6. Values check and soften list

**What the child learns, by action.** Helping others (carrying gently); moving together; leaving real eggs alone (Tisha steps back). Nothing teaches fear, shame, bribery or bragging, provided the rows below hold.

| Risk | Gentle replacement |
|---|---|
| Storm or dark night as the cause | A breezy, sunny afternoon, with no thunder and no dark. If it ends at dusk, a pink-gold sky. |
| Egg in danger (cold, cracking, "hurry!") | The egg is warm, happy and tapping a cheer. **The glow only rises or holds, and never dims**, so it can never feel like the viewer's failure. |
| Tisha lost or alone (separation anxiety is real at 3-4 [S weak] https://cehhs.utk.edu/klass/2018/06/12/the-preschool-drama-separation-anxiety/ ) | Footprints always show the way; an animal friend walks her to each border; the narrator and the viewer are always with her. |
| Mama peahen frantic or crying | Show her calm, humming and close, in the first minute. Her soft call is the compass ("Hear Mama? This way!"). |
| Animal as villain or thief | None. No snakes, jackals, mongooses or crows, even for comedy. |
| Falls, splashes, something scary near a big animal | None. The elephant never stomps near Tisha; her own stomp is play. |
| Water: WHO ranks drowning among the leading causes of death for ages 1-4 [S] https://who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/drowning ; YouTube bars content that encourages minors in dangerous activity [S] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801999 | Tisha stays on dry grass beside pond and lake. Lily-pad jumps are painted circles on the ground. Never wading, never crossing water. |
| Humiliation | Every animal is skilled and kind; no one is laughed at. |
| Bribery, bragging, ranking | The reward is the hatching, not a toy or treat. "Shabash" praises trying, never "best". No scores, no "beat the others", no weight, calorie or body-shape talk. |

**Never-show list.** Deity images or sacred symbols on mats, floors or footwear; shripada-style footprints; the egg on an altar or lotus; real feathers collected; anyone taking home a wild egg or animal; a working or temple elephant; monkeys stealing; scary snakes; Tisha near water alone.
