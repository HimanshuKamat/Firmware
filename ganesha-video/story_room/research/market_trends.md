# Market trends: preschool and toddler YouTube, 2025 to 2026 (India and diaspora lens)

Researched 2026-10-07. Tags: [S] source-backed, [I] my inference, [U] could not verify. The search tool is US-only, so India data is thin and often secondary.

## 1. What is growing, what is saturated

**Audience reality [S].** Pew (May 2025 survey): 62% of parents of under-2s and 84% of parents of 2-to-4s say the child watches YouTube; daily use rose from 24% to 35% (under 2) and 38% to 51% (ages 2-4) since 2020; nearly half say their child met inappropriate videos. https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/10/08/how-parents-manage-screen-time-for-kids/ . Common Sense 2025: online video is now the top screen activity for ages 0-8; short-form rose from 1 to 14 min/day (figures from search snippets). https://www.commonsensemedia.org/research/the-2025-common-sense-census-media-use-by-kids-zero-to-eight . YouTube held 13.8% of US TV viewing in May 2026 (Nielsen), so long-form 16:9 is watched on the living-room TV. https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/416872/streaming-viewing-share-hits-record-high-in-may-n.html

**Reach leaders [S].** US homes with 0-5s, Dec 2023-Nov 2024: Bluey 24%, Disney Jr 23%, Ms Rachel, Super Simple Songs and CoComelon 22% each. https://digital-i.com/news-articles/press-release-bluey-is-top-dog-on-youtube-streaming-in-the-u-s

**Growing**
- YouTube-to-streamer pipeline [S]: Ms Rachel on Netflix (Jan 2025) hit 2.6B minutes by 25 Apr 2025, tied with Mickey Mouse Clubhouse (Luminate). https://luminatedata.com/blog/kids-tvs-unlikely-dominant-duo-ms-rachel-mickey-mouse/ . ChuChu TV licensed four collections to Netflix globally from 6 Aug 2026. https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/news/kids-youtube-channel-chuchutv-with-over-60-billion-views-making-jump-over-to-netflix/ . YouTube viewing by 2-to-11s rose 4% in 2024 (Tubefilter). https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=184310
- Human-led, participatory, speech-building content (Ms Rachel) and sensory content (Hey Bear Sensory, 2.9M subscribers, 2B+ views) [S, weak, search snippet]. https://www.romper.com/parenting/hey-bear-sensory-youtube-dancing-veggie-videos
- "Slow TV" parent preference for calm shows (Bluey, Sesame) [S, weak, trade/lifestyle press]. https://www.aol.com/slow-tv-movement-why-parents-091010241.html

**Saturated or fading**
- Fast nursery-rhyme compilations: CoComelon's Netflix viewership fell nearly 60% (H1 2023 to H2 2024); Netflix did not renew and it moves to Disney+ in 2027, though the YouTube channel still averages about 2B views a month. https://www.netinfluencer.com/disney-acquires-preschool-series-cocomelon-as-netflix-exits-due-to-viewership-decline/ [S]
- Indian rhymes-plus-toys: nine of the top ten Indian kids channels carry "Toys" or "Family" tags (section 2) [S]; a new rhyme channel would face 30-100M-subscriber incumbents [I].
- AI "educational" filler and toddler Shorts (section 3).

**Formats, lengths, series** [U]: no reliable data on optimal length found. [I] Gentle 4-8 min stories suit parent-chosen wind-down viewing. Recurring characters are the proven structure: ChuChu uses four and about 50% original songs. https://www.licensingcorner.com/interview-youtube-pre-school-sensation-chuchu-tvs-ceo-creative-director-vinoth-chandar/ [S, undated interview]

## 2. India specifics

**Top Indian kids channels (HypeAuditor, Oct 2026) [S, aggregator]:** ChuChu TV 98.3M; Infobells Hindi 72.9M; JioHotstar Kids 58.6M; PowerKids TV 50.9M; Jingle Toons 46.4M; Kids Channel India 43.7M; WowKidz 41.4M; Infobells Telugu 37.7M, Tamil 36.4M; ZappyToons Hindi 33.7M. https://hypeauditor.com/top-youtube-all-india/?p=12

**Languages [S]:** Infobells runs six language channels (150M combined subscribers). https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2024/01/11/dcm77-infobells.html . WowKidz: 34 channels, 18 languages, 60M+ (older report: 28 channels, 10 languages, 21M in 2019). https://toybook.com/cosmos-maya-hits-21-million-subscribers-for-wowkidz-launches-regional-channels . Hindi is the most-preferred YouTube language, then Tamil, Telugu, English; 37% prefer neither Hindi nor English (search snippet, page not readable). https://businessworld.in/article/youtube-india-capitalizing-on-growing-demand-for-language-content--178244 [S, weak]. Seven audio tracks gave MrBeast 47M+ Indian subscribers, and a family channel (KL BRO Biju Rithvik) reached 79M+ with stories needing no translation. https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/2025s-biggest-trends-on-youtube-that-gave-india-a-new-shared-vernacular/ [S] [I] Visual-first, low-dialogue storytelling suits our weak lip-sync.

**Mythology and festivals**
- Shemaroo's "Bal Ganesh" main channel: 1.31M subscribers, 631 videos, plus Telugu, Gujarati, Tamil, Kannada and Marathi channels of 160-400K each, built around Ganesha and **Mooshak the mouse** (vidIQ via search snippet, July 2025). https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UC69JwXPSY2pT7beY1Av1Gww [S, weak]. **Flag: our channel name and sidekick overlap an existing franchise [I].**
- *Mahavatar Narsimha* (July 2025): about Rs 300 crore on a Rs 20 crore budget; Hindi gave 75%+ of domestic earnings; Ormax argues it succeeded despite being animated, not because of it, through reverent mythology and family co-viewing; a ten-film series is planned. https://www.ormaxmedia.com/stories/mahavatar-narsimha-mythology-wins-animation-follows.html [S] [I] Reverence is a selling point; the story itself is too intense for ages 3-6.
- English-language, toddler-paced, respectful Ganesha/festival channel with real scale: not found [U].

**Diaspora [S]:** 35.4M overseas Indians, 5.4M in the US. https://www.wam.ae/en/article/15hsmel-indian-diaspora-ranks-world’s-biggest-354-million (search snippet).

**Indian parents [S]:** survey of 3,624 parents of 2-5s (north India, Jul-Oct 2024, published 2025): over 60% of children get 2-4 h/day; no limits and mealtime screens raise risk. https://impressions.manipal.edu/open-access-archive/13079

**Platform money [S]:** Rs 21,000 crore paid to Indian creators in three years; 15,000+ channels exceed 1M subscribers. https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/youtubes-india-bet-inr-21000-crore-paid-out-to-indian-creators-commits-inr-850-crores-to-power-indias-creator-nation/

**Indian law [S]:** IT Rules amendment (in force 20 Feb 2026) requires prominent labels and provenance metadata for realistic synthetic media; it targets content "indistinguishable from reality". https://www.hlc.com/en/publications/india-introduces-mandatory-labelling-for-ai-and-3hour-takedown-for-illegal-content [I] Stylised 3D animation is probably outside it; confirm with counsel. DPDP Rules: children's-data provisions (verifiable parental consent; no tracking or targeted ads at under-18s) apply from 14 May 2027. https://www.amsshardul.com/insight/enforcement-of-the-dpdp-act-and-notification-of-the-dpdp-rules/

**Standing rules [S].** Inauthentic content: character series with distinct storylines per episode and consistent intros are permitted; repeated scenarios with identical outcomes and templated AI are at risk. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 . ## 5. Gaps and opportunities

1. **Calm English festival stories with Indian words intact**: no scaled competitor found [U]; Bal Ganesh and Indian rivals skew Hindi/regional [I].
2. **"What we do at Diwali/Holi/Pongal/Onam" for a 4-year-old**: a 4-minute diaspora explainer.
3. **Story-plus-movement films**: participatory formats work (Ms Rachel); an Indian-myth version not found [U].
4. **Transparent, human-reviewed "slow channel"**: one film per 2-4 weeks, named advisor, "how we make it" note.
5. **Wind-down edition**: 5-8 min, dim palette, lullaby ending.
6. **Multi-language audio tracks** (Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu) on one video.
7. **Toddler-grade "Bal" gods**: Krishna's butter (Janmashtami), Hanuman's sun-fruit, Ganesha and the moon; gentle where *Mahavatar* is intense.
8. **Sibling and grandparent bonds**: Raksha Bandhan, Bhai Dooj, "call Nani/Ajji".
9. **Parent kit**: printable colouring/counting sheet per film; parents say they struggle to manage viewing (Pew: 42% could do better).
10. **Evergreen annual festival films**: re-promote the same Diwali/Ganesh film yearly.
11. **Shorts as teasers only**: toddler Shorts feeds are where slop concentrates.
12. **Licensing path**: ChuChu and Ms Rachel moved from YouTube to Netflix [I].

## How the Story Room should use this

1. **Calendar gate**: every idea brief names its hook date (IST plus US date), script-lock (T-8 weeks) and go-live (T-3 weeks). Diwali (8 Nov) is 32 days out: decide this week or aim at Children's Day and Christmas.
2. **No template rule**: each episode needs a different story problem and ending, stated in one line; recurring characters and one signature chant are fine, repeated structure is not.
3. **Quality over cadence**: maximum one long film per 2 weeks, no batch uploads.
4. **Trust package per film**: named cultural reviewer, safety/fact check, no scary beats, AI-assisted note in the description even though animation is exempt, Made for Kids set correctly.
5. **Brand check before Mooshak is locked**: owner decides on the Shemaroo overlap; do not build channel art around it first.
6. **Movement beat**: every script includes at least two physical prompts, designed with the movement designer.
7. **Re-voice ready**: short sentences, Indian words kept, little reliance on lip-sync.
8. **Compliance and CTA**: if ad revenue matters, apply to the Partner Program before 31 Jan 2027; put next-watch prompts inside the picture because cards and end screens are off on Made for Kids; plan for DPDP from 14 May 2027.
