# Graphics brief: "Ganesha's Big Race" (HyperFrames)

Audience: toddlers 4-5 and their parents on YouTube. 16:9, 1920x1080, 24 fps. Everything here sits on top of (or in front of) a warm 3D-animated picture, so the graphics must look like part of it: soft, rounded, bright, friendly, premium. Think "big-studio kids' channel", not "template".

## Look
- Palette (from 01_script/bible.md, already in `kit.css`): saffron #F28C28, sunset red #D63B2F, marigold gold #F6B21A, peacock teal #1AA6A0, cream #FFF3DC, sky #8FD3F4, meadow green #8CCB6B, rose pink #F4A6B8. Outline colour for cream type: dark red `--ink` #7A1F16.
- Type: **Fredoka** only (`font-weight` 500-700), big and rounded. `.display` gives cream fill + dark-red outline + soft shadow. Minimum text size 64 px (lyrics), headlines 140-200 px, numerals 300+ px.
- Shapes: very rounded corners (40-60 px), soft drop shadows, gold rims, marigold flowers and petals as decoration (`Kit.svg.marigold`, `Kit.petalFall`), small 4-point sparkles (`Kit.sparkBurst`). No hard black, no neon, no thin hairlines (thinnest stroke 6 px).
- Keep it uncluttered: one main idea per overlay, generous margins (keep text inside the central 90% of the frame; nothing closer than 96 px to an edge).

## Motion
- Gentle: soft ease-in/out, back.out pops for numbers/titles, slow floats. Nothing flashes: no luminance flicker, no fade shorter than 0.25 s, no strobing sparkles.
- Text stays on screen at least 3 s (the brief says so). Every text overlay fades out softly.
- Overlays that sit on top of the picture must never cover a character's face. Check the real frames.
- Deterministic only: GSAP tweens on one paused timeline, seeded random (`Kit.rng`), no `Date.now`, no network, no `repeat:-1` without finite duration.

## Toolchain (HyperFrames CLI, already installed; telemetry off; Chrome headless shell ready)
Read first: `~/.claude/skills/hyperframes-core/SKILL.md` (composition contract) and `~/.claude/skills/hyperframes-animation/SKILL.md` (motion). Use `hyperframes-keyframes` only if needed.

Each graphic is its own standalone project: `07_graphics/projects/<name>/index.html`.
1. `cd /home/user/Firmware/ganesha-video/07_graphics && ./sync_shared.sh projects/<name>` copies fonts, GSAP, images and `kit.css`/`kit.js` into the project (so it is self-contained). Do not edit `shared/`. If you need a change to the kit, put your own CSS/JS inside your `index.html`.
2. Write `projects/<name>/index.html` (standalone root, `data-composition-id`, `data-width`/`data-height`, `data-duration`, one paused GSAP timeline registered at `window.__timelines["<id>"]`; `body` background must stay transparent for overlays). Load assets with relative paths (`kit.css`, `kit.js`, `vendor/gsap.min.js`, `img/...`).
3. Validate: `npx hyperframes lint projects/<name>` then `npx hyperframes check projects/<name>` (0 findings). `npx hyperframes snapshot projects/<name> --at 0.5,1.5,...` gives PNG frames on a checker/solid background to eyeball.
4. Render overlays with alpha: `npx hyperframes render projects/<name> --format mov --fps 24 -o renders/<name>.mov` (ProRes 4444, run from `07_graphics`; output path is relative to the project dir if you pass `-o renders/...`; check the printed path). Opaque pieces (intro, shorts end card) render `--format mp4 -q delivery`.
5. **Look at your result on the real picture**: `python3 preview.py projects/<name>/renders/<name>.mov <START_NOINTRO> previews/<name>_check.png t1 t2 t3 ...` (times are seconds from the overlay's own start; START_NOINTRO is in `timing.json`). Open the PNG with the Read tool and judge it: overlap with faces, legibility at phone size (view the sheet small), timing of each cue, safe margins. Iterate until it looks professional. Check at least 6 times per overlay including the first and last 0.5 s.
6. Final: copy the render to `07_graphics/renders/<name>.mov` (or `.mp4`) and save a final contact sheet to `07_graphics/previews/<name>_final.png`.

Timing source of truth: `07_graphics/timing.json` (seconds on the no-intro timeline where shot 02 starts at 0; `start_final` adds the 6 s intro). Cue values are relative to the overlay's own start, so your render must have its cue animations at exactly those relative times, and its length must equal `dur` (set root `data-duration` to it). If a cue seems wrong against the real picture, say so in your report rather than silently changing it.

The actual picture used for previews is `08_edit/work/base_picture_1080_v1.mp4` (shot 02 at 0 s; shot start times: 02:0 03:8 04:14 05:21 06:29 07:37 08:45 09:53 10:63 11:71 12:80 13:88 14:96 15:105 16:113 17:121 18:129 19:137 20:145 21:153 22:161 23:169 24:177 25:186 26:195 27:202 28:211 29:219 30:228 31:236 32:246 33:254). Crossfades are 0.5 s at each cut.

Do not commit anything to git. Do not touch other projects, `shared/`, or files outside `07_graphics/` (except reading). Report back: files written, what you verified, any cue you doubt.
