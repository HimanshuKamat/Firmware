# Cozy Spooky Corner — Halloween Colouring Book Plan

Format: 8 x 8 in square (2400 x 2400 px at 300 dpi), one illustration per page.
Style: cosy, cute, kawaii Halloween. Thick bold black outlines, pure white fill, no grey,
no colour, no hatching or gradients. Big simple shapes, minimal tiny detail. Chubby,
rounded characters with sweet faces. Every page is a full scene filling the page edge to edge.
All characters and compositions are original.

## Title options
1. **Cozy Spooky Corner** (working title)
2. Pumpkin Spice & Everything Nice — A Cosy Halloween Colouring Book
3. Sweet & Spooky — Cute Halloween Scenes to Colour
4. Little Boo Lane — A Cosy Halloween Colouring Book
5. Hocus Pocus Hygge

Recommendation: keep **Cozy Spooky Corner** as the main title with the subtitle
"Cute & Cosy Halloween Scenes to Colour". It says exactly what the book is.

## Front matter
- **Cover (colour):** a round-faced little witch in an oversized hat sitting on a stack of
  pumpkins in a corner of a candlelit room, a chubby black cat in her lap, a small smiling ghost
  peeking over her shoulder, bunting of bats and stars overhead, the title in a soft rounded
  hand-lettered font. Warm palette: pumpkin orange, plum, cream, mint and deep navy.
- **"This book belongs to ____":** a big friendly carved pumpkin holding a blank wooden sign
  with the text and a writing line, two tiny bats and a few candy corns around it. Same line-art
  style as the interior so it can be coloured too.

## Interior pages (18)
1. **Pumpkin patch** — a chubby kid in a scarf and wellies pulling a wagon of round pumpkins through a patch, a scarecrow waving, vines curling across the foreground.
2. **Haunted-but-cute house** — a lopsided little house with a smiling crescent window, a ghost waving from the attic, pumpkins on the porch steps and a crooked picket fence.
3. **Trick-or-treating** — three small kids in costumes (a ghost sheet, a cat, a pumpkin) walking down a lantern-lit path holding candy buckets, houses with glowing jack-o'-lanterns behind them.
4. **Cosy reading nook** — a sleepy bear in a witch hat curled in a big armchair with a book and a blanket, chunky candles on a side table, a stack of books and a window with the moon.
5. **Potion shop** — a round little shopkeeper behind a counter with rows of big bottles with heart and star labels, a hanging scale and a bat sleeping upside down from a shelf.
6. **Pumpkin bakery** — a bakery counter with pumpkin pies, cat-shaped cookies and a tiered cake, a baker mouse in a chef hat, a big "Open" sign shaped like a pumpkin.
7. **Ghost tea party** — three friendly ghosts around a small round table with a teapot, teacups and cupcakes, bunting overhead and a candelabra in the middle.
8. **Bat sleepover** — little bats in pyjamas hanging upside down in a cosy cave room, pillows, a string of star lights, one bat reading a book with a torch.
9. **Costume party** — a hall with balloons and bunting, a kid dressed as a vampire dancing with a kid dressed as a mummy, a snack table with a punch bowl.
10. **Moonlit garden** — a big round moon over a garden with oversized mushrooms, a toad wearing a tiny witch hat on a lily pad, fireflies drawn as little stars and a stone path.
11. **Broom repair shop** — a cheerful owl mechanic with goggles fixing a broom on a workbench, brooms hanging on a wall rack, a toolbox and a "Brooms Fixed Here" sign.
12. **Witch's kitchen** — a little witch stirring a bubbling cauldron, jars of sweets on shelves, a string of drying herbs and a sleepy black cat on the windowsill.
13. **Candy shop** — a shop window filled with giant lollipops, candy jars and wrapped sweets, a striped awning, and a skeleton puppy pressing its nose to the glass.
14. **Scarecrow's picnic** — a smiling scarecrow and a crow friend sharing a picnic blanket in a cornfield, apples, a thermos and a basket, hay bales behind.
15. **Monster bath time** — a fluffy little monster in a clawfoot tub full of bubbles, a rubber duck in a witch hat, towels on a rack and a bat-shaped bathmat.
16. **Pumpkin carving night** — two kids at a kitchen table carving pumpkins, a bowl of seeds, finished jack-o'-lanterns lined up on the counter, a cat batting at a pumpkin stem.
17. **Hot cocoa by the fire** — a vampire bunny and a werewolf puppy in blankets by a fireplace with mugs of cocoa and big marshmallows, stockings shaped like pumpkins on the mantel.
18. **Midnight broom flight** — a little witch and her cat flying on a broom over rooftops and a church spire under a huge moon, round clouds and a few big stars.

## Shared style wording (reused in every generation)
> Black and white colouring book page for kids, cute cosy kawaii Halloween style, thick bold
> clean black outlines of uniform weight, pure white fill everywhere, no grey, no shading, no
> hatching, no gradients, no colour, no solid black areas, large simple rounded shapes, chubby
> characters with sweet happy faces, minimal small detail, full scene filling the whole square
> page edge to edge, white background.
