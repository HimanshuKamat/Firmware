# Prompts

## Shared style block (prepend to every prompt)
High-quality Pixar-like 3D cartoon, soft rounded shapes, big expressive eyes, glossy but friendly materials, soft studio lighting, rainbow-bright saturated (not neon) colors, child-safe, no scary elements, no brand logos, no text unless specified.

## Mascot description block (include in every prompt)
A cheerful gender-neutral 7-year-old explorer kid with curly brown hair, big warm brown eyes, round friendly face, warm medium-brown skin, yellow overalls over a white tee, rainbow-striped sneakers, small teal explorer backpack with red buckle. Accompanied by a tiny mint-green baby dino with a round belly, oversized eyes and small orange spots.

## 1. Hero mascot (lock the look first)
[Style block] [Mascot block] Full-body standing front view, big friendly smile, waving, plain cream background, centered, character reference sheet quality.

## 2. Poses (use approved hero image as reference; keep identical character)
Same prompt prefix, then one of:
1. happy – big smile, hands on hips, standing proudly
2. excited – jumping with arms up, mouth open in joy
3. thinking – finger on chin, looking up, small thought bubble with a lightbulb
4. exercising – doing a jumping jack, energetic, motion lines
5. waving – friendly wave toward the viewer
6. reading – sitting holding an open storybook, dino peeking in
7. adventuring – holding binoculars with a folded map, dino beside
8. sleeping – curled up cozy with a pillow and blanket, dino asleep too
Plain cream background, full body, consistent proportions.

## 3. Logo / avatar (1:1, 800x800)
[Style block] [Mascot block, reference image] Close-up of the mascot's smiling face and shoulders inside a circular rainbow-ring badge, cream center, bold and simple so it reads at very small size. No text (or "[CHANNEL NAME]" in chunky rounded font if provided).

## 4. Banner (16:9, 2560x1440)
[Style block] [Mascot block, reference image] Wide adventure scene: bright rainbow arcing over a sunny sky, rolling green hills with a winding path, mascot and dino standing in the center. Keep all important elements within the central area; extra scenery on left and right; calm empty space at the top and bottom. Optional text "[CHANNEL NAME]" centered in chunky rounded letters.

## 5. Thumbnail templates (16:9, 1280x720)
Mascot on the left third, large empty space on the right for title text, thick white outline around the mascot, high contrast.
- Learning: Sky Blue background with floating letters, numbers and a tiny planet.
- Exercise: Orange Pop background with motion lines; mascot mid jumping jack.
- Story: Grape Purple and Leaf Green background with a castle/forest map and sparkles.
