# Kids Channel Brand Kit

Everything needed to keep a kids' YouTube channel (ages 2–8: learning, exercise, stories, adventures) visually consistent. Hand this whole folder to any agent making videos, newsletters or Instagram posts.

## Contents
- `brand-guide.md` – mascot, palette (hex), typography, specs, do/don't. **Read first.**
- `mascot/` – character sheet and 8 poses (happy, excited, thinking, exercising, waving, reading, adventuring, sleeping).
- `logo/` – 800x800 avatar.
- `banner/` – 2560x1440 channel banner.
- `thumbnails/` – 1280x720 templates and per-topic variants (learning, exercise, story).
- `prompts/` – the exact prompts used, for regenerating or extending assets consistently.

## Status
Guides and prompts are written. Images are not generated yet: that step needs `GEMINI_API_KEY` available in the session (see `prompts/generation-instructions.md`).

## Using the kit
1. Give the agent this folder and tell it to follow `brand-guide.md`.
2. Always attach the approved mascot image from `mascot/` as a reference for new artwork.
3. Fill in the channel name and tagline in `brand-guide.md` once chosen.
