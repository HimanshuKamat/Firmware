# Brand Guide – Kids Learning & Adventure Channel

Channel name: **[CHANNEL NAME – TBD]**  ·  Tagline: **[TAGLINE – TBD]**
Audience: ages 2–8 and their parents. Content: learning, exercise, stories and adventures.

## Brand personality
Curious, energetic, kind, playful. Always encouraging, never scary, never sarcastic. Every video feels like a bright adventure with a friend.

## Mascot
- **Explorer kid** (gender-neutral, ~7 years old): curly brown hair, big warm brown eyes, round friendly face, warm medium-brown skin tone, wide smile.
- **Outfit (never changes):** yellow overalls, white short-sleeve tee, rainbow-striped sneakers, small teal explorer backpack with a red buckle.
- **Sidekick:** a tiny baby dino, mint green, round belly, oversized eyes, small orange spots, stubby tail.
- **Style:** high-quality Pixar-like 3D cartoon, soft rounded shapes, glossy but friendly materials, soft studio lighting, subtle rim light.
- **Consistency rule:** same face, proportions, hair and outfit in every asset. Always attach the approved mascot image as a reference when generating anything new.

### Poses (mascot/)
1. happy  2. excited  3. thinking  4. exercising (jumping jacks)  5. waving  6. reading  7. adventuring (binoculars/map)  8. sleeping

## Color palette
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ruby Red | #FF4B5C | accents, buttons, "action" |
| Orange Pop | #FF8A1F | exercise content, highlights |
| Sunshine Yellow | #FFD43B | mascot overalls, logo base |
| Leaf Green | #3DCB6A | nature, stories, sidekick |
| Sky Blue | #35A7FF | backgrounds, learning content |
| Grape Purple | #8E5CF7 | magic, story content |
| Cream | #FFF8E7 | neutral background |
| Ink Navy | #1F2A55 | text and outlines |

Rainbow-bright but saturated, not neon. Keep plenty of cream or white space for toddlers' visual comfort.

## Typography
Chunky rounded sans-serif (suggested: Fredoka, Baloo 2, or Nunito Black). Title text: Ink Navy fill with thick white outline. Large, short words (max 4 per thumbnail).

## Asset specs
| Asset | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Logo / avatar | 800x800 | mascot face on rainbow circle, readable at 98px |
| Banner | 2560x1440 | key content inside central 1235x338 safe area |
| Thumbnail | 1280x720 | mascot on left or right third, empty space for title |

### Thumbnail topic variants
- **Learning:** Sky Blue background, ABC/number/science props.
- **Exercise:** Orange Pop background, motion lines, energetic pose.
- **Story/Adventure:** Grape Purple + Leaf Green background, map/forest/castle props.

## Do
- Big expressive eyes and clear emotions.
- Rounded shapes, thick readable outlines.
- Positive, inclusive, diverse supporting characters.

## Don't
- No scary, dark, violent or realistic-weapon imagery.
- No third-party brands, logos or copyrighted characters.
- No clutter near the mascot's face; no tiny text.
- Don't change the mascot's outfit, colors or proportions.

## Reuse guidance for other agents
For videos, newsletters and Instagram posts: use assets from this folder, follow the palette and type rules above, and keep the mascot identical. Square (1080x1080) and portrait (1080x1350) social posts should reuse the mascot poses on Cream or a single palette color background.
